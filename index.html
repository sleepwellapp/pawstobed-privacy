Paws to Bed — Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 12, 2026

Paws to Bed (Android package com.goodapps.pawstobed) is a puzzle game published by GoodApps Studio (“we”). This page explains, in plain words, what the game keeps on your device, what it can send, and the choices you have.

In short

  • No account, no sign-in. Your progress stays on your device.
  • Gameplay statistics and crash reports go to Google Firebase only if you choose “Yes, happy to”.
  • From level 35, the game may show an ad between two levels, never after a failed level. Ads are served by Google AdMob, which collects some data on its own (section 5). In Europe, the UK and Switzerland, Google asks for your consent first.
  • The optional “No Ads” purchase removes those ads for good. Payment is handled by Google Play; we never see your payment details.
  • Never your name, email or contacts. We never sell data.

1. What stays on your device

The game saves your progress in the app’s private storage: level reached, coins, helps, lives, the cats’ house, daily quests, sound and music settings, and your answer to the statistics question with the date you gave it (the record of your choice). We have no game server and never receive this file. If your phone’s system backup is on, it may keep a copy in your own Google account; we have no access to it. Uninstalling the game or clearing its data deletes everything.

2. Statistics, if you say yes

If you agree, the game uses Google Analytics for Firebase and Firebase Crashlytics:

  • Game events: a level started, won or failed (level number, attempt, duration, helps used, hearts left), a help used, coins spent, a purchase made (which item and at which level, never the price or the receipt).
  • Events recorded automatically by Firebase: first launch, session start, time in the app, app or system update, uninstall.
  • Technical information: device brand and model, Android version, app version, language.
  • Random identifiers created at installation (app instance ID, Crashlytics and Firebase installation IDs). They change if you reinstall the game or clear its data.
  • Approximate location: country and region, estimated by Google from your IP address. For users in the EU, the UK and Switzerland, Google Analytics does not log or store the IP address.
  • Crash reports: the technical trace of the crash, the device and app state at that moment, and the level and screen you were on.

Why: to fix crashes, to tune how hard the levels are, and to see what players enjoy. Firebase collects no advertising ID, and these statistics are not used for advertising. Legal basis: your consent (Article 6(1)(a) GDPR).

3. Before you answer, and after “No thanks”

  • The game starts with all statistics switched off. Until you answer, nothing is sent; events from the current session wait in memory only and are discarded unless you say yes during that session. A crash report recorded before your answer is deleted.
  • After “No thanks”, all statistics stop at once and crash reports waiting on the device are deleted.
  • Closing the card without answering decides nothing.

4. Change your mind at any time

Open Settings (the button at the bottom left of the game) and tap the line “Share stats”: the question opens again. Your new choice applies immediately, and nothing in the game changes either way.

5. Ads (Google AdMob)

From level 35, the game may show a full-screen ad between two levels: never after a failed level, never before level 35, and at most one every few minutes. The ads are served by Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK). To show ads, measure them and prevent fraud, this SDK collects and shares with Google: your IP address (which gives an approximate location), your interactions with the ads (launches, taps, video views), diagnostic information about the app and the SDK, and device identifiers (the advertising ID and the app set ID). This data is encrypted in transit.

In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, Google’s consent form opens before any ad request; you choose whether ads may be personalised. You can reopen it from Settings. Everywhere, you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone’s settings (Google › Ads).

Google uses this data under its own policy: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

6. The “No Ads” purchase

The optional “No Ads” purchase removes the ads between levels for good. It is processed by Google Play under Google’s terms: we never see or keep your payment details. Your phone keeps the record of the purchase, and Google Play restores it if you reinstall the game with the same Google account.

7. How long data is kept

  • Analytics: detailed event data is kept for 2 months, then deleted; aggregated reports contain no identifier.
  • Crashlytics: crash reports are kept for 90 days.
  • Ads: Google keeps ad data according to its own policy.

8. Who processes the data, and where

For statistics, Google acts on our behalf as a service provider (“data processor”), under the Firebase terms. For ads, Google also uses the data for its own purposes, as described in section 5. Data may be processed in any country where Google has facilities, with the safeguards set out in Google’s terms. We do not share data with anyone else, and we never sell it.

More: Privacy and Security in Firebase · Google Privacy Policy

9. Permissions

The game uses the internet connection (statistics, ads, purchase) and the advertising ID permission added by the ads SDK. It never accesses your contacts, files, photos, location, microphone or camera.

10. Children

Paws to Bed is not designed for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children. The game never asks for an age or a name. If you believe a child’s data has been sent, write to us.

11. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR), you can ask to access, correct or delete your data, or object to its use. You can withdraw your consent at any time in Settings, without losing anything in the game. Because our statistics contain no name or contact details, we usually cannot tell which data is yours; write to us and we will do what we can. You can also complain to your data protection authority (in France, the CNIL).

12. Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date at the top.

El juego guarda tu progreso en el almacenamiento privado de la app: nivel alcanzado, monedas, ayudas, vidas, la casa de los gatos, misiones del día, ajustes de sonido y música, y tu respuesta a la pregunta de las estadísticas con su fecha (la constancia de tu elección). No tenemos servidor de juego y nunca recibimos este archivo. Si la copia de seguridad del sistema está activada, puede guardar una copia en tu propia cuenta de Google; no tenemos acceso a ella. Desinstalar el juego o borrar sus datos lo elimina todo.

Si aceptas, el juego usa Google Analytics para Firebase y Firebase Crashlytics:

  • Información técnica: marca y modelo del dispositivo, versión de Android, versión de la app, idioma.

Abre los Ajustes (el botón abajo a la izquierda del juego) y toca la línea «Compartir estadísticas»: la pregunta vuelve a abrirse. Tu nueva elección se aplica al momento, y nada cambia en el juego.
